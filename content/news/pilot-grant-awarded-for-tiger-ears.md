The lab has been awarded a pilot grant from the UCSF Resource Evaluation and Allocation Committee to support TIGER EARS! This proof-of-concept project, “Digital Phenotyping of Adolescent Depression: Toward Scalable Biomarkers,” will test whether digitally derived behaviors obtained from passive sensing and keyboard smartphone data map onto dissociable phenotypes.

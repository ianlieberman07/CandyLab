Anurima Mummaneni (she/her) is a rising third-year Ph.D. candidate in NSIDP at UCLA. She graduated from the University of Chicago in 2024 with a B.S. in Neuroscience and worked with Dr. Monica Rosenberg applying predictive modeling to large data sets (ABCD, HCP). She is interested in parsing neurodevelopmental trajectories for adolescents and in developing predictive models of depression. Outside of the lab, Anu enjoys hiking, dancing, and looking for the best boba in LA.

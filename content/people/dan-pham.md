Dan is a rising fourth-year at the University of California, Los Angeles and is interested in adolescent mental health and culturally responsive approaches to supporting youth well-being. Her research interests include how family relationships, stress, and social environments shape adolescent development. In the future, She hopes to pursue graduate training and build a career that bridges research, clinical practice, and community-based initiatives to support adolescents and young adults in Vietnam.Outside the lab, she serves as the Executive Director of Active Minds, where she works to promote mental health awareness and create supportive spaces for students. In her free time, she enjoys journaling, video editing, curating playlists, and exploring new food and drinks!

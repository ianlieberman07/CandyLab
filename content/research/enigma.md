Dr. Ho collaborates with researchers around the globe through the [ENIGMA consortium](http://enigma.ini.usc.edu/) to identify the effects of depression and stress on brain morphometry and to examine sex differences and age-related effects on the brain.[Subcortical shape alterations in major depressive disorder: Findings from the ENIGMA Major Depressive Disorder Working Group](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1002/hbm.24988)[Childhood Maltreatment and Deviations from Normative Brain Structure: Results from 3,711 Individuals from the ENIGMA MDD and ENIGMA PTSD](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.04.657717v1.full.pdf)[Other ENIGMA publications](https://candylab.psych.ucla.edu/publications/enigma/)

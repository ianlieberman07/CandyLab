MR imaging is currently not an accessible technology but smartphones are. EARS is a pioneering smartphone app that leverages data collected seamlessly from naturalistic phone usage (including social media use, keyboard content, and geolocation) in order to infer cognitive and affective states relevant for depression risk. The goal of TIGER EARS is to integrate digital phenotypes with neurobiological phenotypes in order to move toward scalable biomarkers of depression. TIGER EARS is being conducted in collaboration with [Dr. Nicholas Allen](https://psychology.uoregon.edu/profile/nallen3/) at the University of Oregon and founder of [Ksana Health](https://www.ksanahealth.com/).[Neural correlates of depression-related smartphone language use in adolescents](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44277-024-00009-6)

Prospective Study Coordinators

We are looking for a reliable, organized, self-directed, and detail-oriented study coordinator with a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Psychology, Neuroscience, or a related field to join our team this summer (ideal start date: August 1, 2026). This role will require conducting clinical assessments and assisting with neuroimaging scans and biospecimen collection, with flexible availability on weeknights and occasional weekends to accommodate participants’ schedules. Please e-mail uclacandylabapps@gmail.com by July 5, 2026 with a PDF of your CV that is named XX_YYYY_CRC2026.pdf (where XX refers to your surname and YYYY refers to the year you have graduated from college) with the subject heading “CANDY Lab Coordinator” if you are interested in joining. Please include the names and contact information for at least two professional references in the body of your e-mail and your availability for the next three weeks in case we decide to potentially schedule an in-person interview with you.

Prospective Psychology Graduate Students

The application cycle for 2026 in [Clinical Psychology](https://www.psych.ucla.edu/graduate/areas-of-study/clinical-psychology/prospective-clinical-area-applicants/) is over. The deadline for applying for the 2027 cycle will be November 1, 2026. Dr. Ho will only accept a student who has a strong background in statistics, programming, and fMRI/MRS/ASL analyses; interests in affective science and/or developmental psychology are strongly encouraged. Because of the volume of e-mails she receives, Dr. Ho will not be able to respond to inquiries about graduate school applications. Dr. Ho will NOT be able to support international students unless they have their own fundin*g***.

Prospective Neuroscience Graduate Students

Dr. Ho is a faculty member of the [Interdepartmental Ph.D. program in Neuroscience](https://grad.ucla.edu/programs/david-geffen-school-of-medicine/neuroscience/) at UCLA and typically accepts at least one rotation student a year. If you are already admitted or enrolled in the program and are interested in rotating in Dr. Ho’s lab, please send an e-mail with your CV, a brief description of your research interests, and your meeting availability for the next two weeks.

Prospective Postdoctoral Researchers

Potential postdoctoral researchers who are interested in applying for external funding to work with Dr. Ho are encouraged to contact her. The ideal candidates are those who have a strong background in human MRI and who are eager to convert large-scale analyses of neuroimaging data to publishable products *or*who are clinically trained and would like to lead training on a variety of clinical instruments. Dr. Ho will NOT be able to sponsor H1B visas.Please e-mail Dr. Ho with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

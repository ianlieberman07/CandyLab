# Editing the CANDY Lab website

Written for Dr. Ho. You will never need a terminal, and you never need to
understand any code. If something here does not match what you see on screen,
the site has changed and this page has not — say so and it will be fixed.

---

## Signing in

Go to **candylab.psych.ucla.edu/admin** and sign in with GitHub.

You need a free GitHub account, and someone has to add that account to the
site's repository once. After that, signing in is one click.

Down the left-hand side you will see everything you can change:

| | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Lab Members** | Everyone currently in the lab |
| **Lab Alumni** | Everyone who has left |
| **Studies** | TIGER, BRIDGE, WAVES and the rest |
| **News** | Announcements |
| **Pages** | Join, Participate, Resources, and any page you make |
| **Publications** | The paper list |
| **Lab Gallery photos** | The gallery page |
| **Pet photos** | The pets page |
| **Site Details** | Lab name, email, address |

---

## The one thing to know

**Every save is permanent but nothing is ever lost.** Each time you click
Publish, the site keeps a dated copy of exactly what changed. Any edit can be
undone later, including deletions. So you cannot break the site by trying
something.

After you publish, the website takes **about a minute** to update. Refresh the
page if you do not see the change immediately.

## Adding a new person to the lab

1. Click **Lab Members**, then **New Lab member**.
2. Fill in their name and title.
3. Under **Which heading do they appear under?**, pick their group — Graduate
   Students, Volunteer Research Assistants, and so on. This is what decides
   where they show up on the People page.
4. Drag in a photo, or skip it. Without a photo the card shows their initials
   and looks fine — it will not look broken.
5. Write their bio in the **Biography** box at the bottom.
6. Click **Publish**.

### About the "Photo description" box

Under every photo there is a **Photo description** box. This is read aloud to
people who are blind and browse using a screen reader. For a headshot you can
leave it blank — the site writes "Portrait of *their name*" for you.

For anything that is not a simple headshot, write a short sentence describing
what is in the picture.

## Moving someone to Alumni

This is one field. Do **not** delete them and retype them in the alumni list.

1. Open the person in **Lab Members**.
2. Change **Currently in the lab?** to **No — move to Lab Alumni**.
3. Publish.

They move to the Alumni page immediately and keep their photo and everything
you have written. To bring someone back, do the same in reverse from the **Lab
Alumni** list.

Once they are in Alumni, it is worth opening them and filling in:

- **Years in the lab** — e.g. `2023-2025`
- **What they did here** — e.g. `Undergraduate RA`
- **Where they are now** — e.g. `Ph.D. student at UT Austin`

Leave **Where they are now** blank if you do not know. The site simply leaves
it off rather than guessing.

### Hiding someone instead of deleting them

Every person has a **Show this person on the site?** switch. Turning it off
removes them from the website but keeps everything you wrote, so you can put
them back with one click. Prefer this to deleting.

## Adding a paper

1. Click **Publications** → **Publication list**.
2. Click **Add Publication** at the top of the list.
3. Fill in title, authors, journal and year.
4. **Authors:** put asterisks around a lab member's name to show it in bold, the
   way the old site did — `Smith J, *Ho TC*, Gotlib IH`.
5. **Link to the paper:** paste the DOI or publisher link. If there is not one,
   leave it blank — the site then offers visitors a search link rather than a
   dead end.
6. **Free to read?** Tick this **only** if anyone can read the whole paper
   without paying or logging in. This is what powers the "Free to read" filter
   and the little teal dot, so it is worth getting right.
7. **Free copy at a different address:** only if the free version lives
   somewhere else — a PubMed Central page, for instance. Leave it blank when
   the link you already pasted is the free one.
8. Publish.

**Number** is just an internal ordering number. Use one higher than the highest
already there.

### If the free version is only a preprint

Tick **The free copy is a preprint**. The site then labels it "Free preprint"
rather than "Read it free", so nobody is told the published paper is free when
it is not.

## Adding a news item

**News** → **New News item**. A headline and a date is a complete announcement —
the text box underneath is optional, and most of the existing items do not use
it.

The three most recent automatically appear on the home page.

---

## Adding or editing a study

**Studies** → **New Study**. The study appears on the Research page and in the
list on the home page automatically; you do not have to add it anywhere else.

- **Short summary** is what shows on the card. One or two sentences.
- **Status tag** is optional — "Coming soon", "Data analysis stage". Leave it
  blank for a study that is simply running.
- **Study image** can be a logo. Small images are shown at their real size
  rather than stretched, so a logo will not go blurry.

---

## Editing an ordinary page

**Pages** holds Join, Participate, Resources, Lab Values and so on. Click one,
edit the **Text** box, publish.

To make a new page, click **New Page**. It gets its own web address and is laid
out in the same style as everything else.

- **Show in the menu at the top?** — the top menu holds about seven items
  comfortably. Pages turned off here still appear in the footer, so nothing
  becomes unreachable.
- **Draft** — tick it while you are still writing. The page stays off the
  website until you untick it.

---

## Changing the email address or contact details

**Site Details**. What you put here appears in the footer and on the home page.
Anything you leave blank is simply left off the site.

---

## Formatting text

The text boxes have buttons for **bold**, *italic*, headings and links. Use
them rather than typing symbols.

To add a link: select the words you want to be the link, click the link button,
paste the address.

Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The page looks wrong after an edit.** Every change is reversible. Ask, and
  the previous version can be restored in under a minute.
- **The admin will not load.** Usually a sign-in problem. Reload first; if it
  persists, send the address of the page you were on.
- **A photo looks blurry.** The original was probably small. The site never
  enlarges a photo, because that only makes it blurrier — a larger original is
  the fix.
- **You see a coloured notice on a page saying it is a placeholder.** That is
  the **Mark as incomplete** switch. Untick it once the text is final.
